`TL;DR:` _July Iceland trip, drove south coast out to Diamond Beach and back while stopping at cool sites along on the way. Highly recommend the country, but plan accordingly for weather & prices._

## Key notes

- `$$$` - food and much else is hella expensive, even at crappy places. Plan accordingly, grocery store meals or similar. Costco food court still cheap 😎
- [Kringum app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.thoroddsen.kringum&hl=en-US) was recommended by a local for finding more spots than ChatGPT will tell you about
- 🌧️ unless you're lucky with weather, you need an actual waterproof rain jacket. Good chance your current one doesn't cut it. Waterproof pants could be considered, as well.
    - _note: we got super lucky and had a week of basically all dry sunny weather, which I don't think is the norm._
- 🚗 driving is easy, feels like rural USA in most places.
- Icelandic candy: not a fan. Soooo much licorice flavoring
- It was still light past 11, so consider stretching your days. We did a few hikes at like 10pm when it was less busy.
- neat pizzeria and hostel in a very remote location Hrifunes Pizzeria. Could be a cool place to use as a basecamp for a night
- Become Icelandic, chug `Collab` energy drinks!
- Some places we missed but which are worth considering:
    - Glymur, multiple friends said it was highlight of their trip. _"can't emphasize enough how cool Glymur is" - Carlos_
    - thermal river on the south loop - bring booze & food & chill a while, and there are various hot springs scattered around the ring road
    - Tomato Farm restaurant, but books out super far in advance. _"I still dream about that soup" - Dhushyanth_
    - Beer spa in Akureyri
    - Glacier hikes
    - Kerið Crater, could hit this on the way back from south loop if you want
    - 🙄 `The Penis Museum`. I'm told this is less gimmicky and surpisingly not a bad time from skeptical folks, but didn't go myself.
    - 🧑‍🎤 We wanted to find a Icelandic metal show/bar, but they were all closed during the weekdays.

## Full Itinerary

4 people driving in one car through Iceland, Fri night - Thurs afternoon.

### Fri

- 9pm flight, 6 hours duration
- _⚠️ warning: icelandair does not serve free meals, bring your own!_

### Sat

- 9am arrive, hung out in Reykjavik
- Hallgrímskirkja, wander downtown, check out their parliament square, etc.
- We stumbled on a punk concert in a grassy square, maybe you will too!
- _⚠️ stay awake until at least 8pm_
- We stumbled on a super quirky townie bar `The Icelandic Rockbar`. I wouldn't recommend going out of your way far, but it can't hurt to stop for one drink with the _interesting..._ regulars - all 5 of them on a Sat night.

### Sun

- Drove to stay near `Kirkjubæjarklaustur`, stopping many times along the way at natural wonders
- Great park `Thingvellir`, some people snorkel in the freezing water with tour guides! Beautiful valley and cool to see historic seat of parliament
- `Geysir`, Strokkur geyser erupts every few minutes. Lots of little ones bubbling everywhere as well.
- `Gullfoss` - huge ole waterfall
- `Seljalandsfoss`: waterfall you can walk behind. Plus another inside a cool cave, `Gljúfrabúi`, which you can get to from the same parking spot.
- `Skogafoss`, cool viewpoint you can climb to at the top and see another smaller waterfall
- Vik is a neat little town.

### Mon

- `Diamond Beach` & `Jökulsárlón Glacier Lagoon`
    - you can go on tours of the ice caves and on the boats, but honestly feel like I got 93% of the experience from on shore.
- Stop at various glacier viewpoints, it's huge and visible from many spots
- `Fjallsárlón` - similar to Glacier Lagoon, but less busy. Just down the road and also very cool. Heard icebergs calving off the wall here & almost saw one flip (but we had just turned away & missed it 😔)

### Tues

- `Reynisfjara Black Sand Beach`, lots of puffins. Multiple stops you can do in the area to hike around cool cliffs
- `Dyrhólaey Nature Reserve & Puffin Cliffs`
- Drive back to Reykjavik

### Wed

- Explore Reykjavik more.
- `Sky lagoon` - pricey, but fun experience. A little bit like a factory version of a spa experience. Seemed better than Blue Lagoon. We spent like 4 hours here, but seem people seem to rush through the Ritual you can only do once, which is a choice.
- ‼️SUPER RECOMMEND `Mama Reykjavik VEGAN RESTAURANT`. Such wholesome people, really good food.
- 👎`Íslenski Barinn`: we wanted to try Icelandic dishes like whale, but they give you such tiny portions here we each only got 1 bite. If you're going to try whale, shark, puffin, etc., look somewhere else.

### Thurs

- volcano hikes near KEF. Wished we left more time for volcano hikes, but also no live magma when we went.
- `Bláalónsvegur Road Lavafield` is a cool stop on the way to Grindavik, you can walk around on the old road which got partially covered with lava & had to be re-routed.
- `Nátthagi Valley` was easy walk from the parking place to see newer lava flows.
- `Grindavik` is surreal to drive through now that it's been mostly evacuated.
- The volcano levees are a cool feat of human engineering.
- flew home afternoon

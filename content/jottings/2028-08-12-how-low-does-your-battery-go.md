_**Can you let your phone battery drop to 10% without stressing?**_

I have a hypothesis that you can learn about a person's core traits by checking for a very basic signal: whether they are comfortable letting their phone battery drop to 10% or less before they go looking for a charger[^1].

In my mind, these signal a person with an Abundance mindset, optimistic that there will always be something nearby which can solve their problem. It also signals a high risk-tolerance, where they are either ok with the consequences, or just don't even think about them. I see this on Zoom calls at work  all the time, where someone is presenting a critical presentation with a battery sitting at 5%, and it's all I can do to not scream my anxiety to the heavens.

On the opposite end, you have someone like me, who is coming at it with a Scarcity mindset (though i'm working on growing out of this!). If my phone drops below even 60%, I start looking for a charger to top it back off. It shows up in other areas of life as well, like refilling the filtered water jug, even when it's 80% full. It's the _"Just in Case"_ approach to preparedness, with the assumption that a drought could be just around the corner (of many types, could be a "drought" of no phone chargers, for example). It's the kind of person who embodies the mantra _"you'd rather have it and not need it, than need it and not have it"_.

What should you do with this information? That's up to you! At a baseline, it doesn't hurt to better understand your friends/family/coworker's motivations.


[^1]: It might also work to ask how many times in the last year they've had their phone die.

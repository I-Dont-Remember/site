> _You can lead a horse to make an anonymous feedback form, but how do you get the other horses to fill it out? \
> \- a wise sage_
>
I really enjoyed reading [Cate Hall's book "You Can Just Do Things"](https://usefulfictions.substack.com/about) and loved the concept of getting anonymous feedback to help notice personal blind spots. It's something I've noodled on before, so it immediately resonated when she suggested a clear action step of creating an [anonymous feedback form](https://www.admonymous.co/kevin-quinn). With that done, I'm realizing there's a bigger issue than me making a form: discoverability.

The people I know in real life don't check my website, Twitter(X), or any other online profile, so the number of organic submissions I'll get rounds to 0.

If I contact people directly though, it doesn't seem anonymous. If you kill the anonymity, then I'll just get a bunch of nice sounding words which don't actually help me grow to be a better human & friend.

The very meh solution I've got for now is to do a message blast to many people at once, and make it clear it's a big group, to try to preserve the sense of anonymity _(the reality doesn't matter as much as someone's perception, in this case)_. That still feels a bit spammy, unfortunately. This will have to be a work in progress idea for now. 

Luckily there's my [anonymous feedback form](https://www.admonymous.co/kevin-quinn) now if you have any ideas :)

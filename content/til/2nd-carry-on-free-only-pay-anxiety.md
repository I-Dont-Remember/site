Til: if you're willing to sit with discomfort and a bit of anxiety, you can get a 2nd carry-on for free. The strategy seems to be bringing two carry-on size bags, one that is plausibly smaller than the other, then feign trying to make it fit under the seat until you finally request a stewardess's help. Woman next to us on a flight did this and it worked, no one charged her. No way it works on every flight, especially if they run out of overhead bin space, but it's an option if you want to risk it and count on the crew caring more about getting the plane off the ground quickly than making you pay.
